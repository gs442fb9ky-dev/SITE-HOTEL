## Restaurant and kitchen

### Verified identity

An on-site restaurant is explicitly present. It is titled “Our Restaurant” and “Restaurant” on the official site. No separate proper restaurant name is provided. Suitable page label: **Dining** or **The Restaurant at Surya Shanti**. The latter is a descriptive label, not a claimed registered restaurant name.

**Buddha Bar** is a verified name for the bar, distinct from the restaurant.

### Cuisine and setting

The hotel says its Balinese chefs prepare fusion cuisine using local ingredients and French know-how, alongside traditional Balinese recipes. The restaurant overlooks the pool and valley. The kitchen makes its own marmalades, bread and ice cream, with fruit from the garden used in its ice cream. Do not broaden this into “all ingredients from our organic garden” or “farm-to-table”: those claims are unsupported.

Faithful editorial copy:

> Balinese ingredients, a French touch. Our chefs bring local produce and French culinary know-how together in a relaxed restaurant overlooking the pool and valley. House-made bread, marmalades and ice cream made with fruit from the garden add a personal touch to the table.

The named kitchen team on the official site is Ibu Gatri, Agung and Tanaya. Restaurant team names are Ketut, Sepi, Dedet and Karsa. These are source facts; current staffing should be confirmed before using names in a prominent team section.
